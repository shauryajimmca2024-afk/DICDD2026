Official Invitation

Dear Sir/Ma'am,

It is with immense pride and sincere gratitude that we invite you to the 10th Edition of DICD – Dialogues in Interventional & Clinical Dermatology, to be held at the beautiful Holymont Resort, Udaipur, from 18th to 20th December.

And today, as we celebrate our 10th milestone edition, we say this with all humility—This journey would not have been possible without YOU.

We Promise You This:

The 10th DICD will be our biggest one yet—in science, scale, faculty and overall experience.

Your belief, your partnership, and your commitment have made DICD one of India's most respected and awaited dermatology platforms. You have not just supported a conference—you have shaped a movement.

As we step into this landmark year, we need your support once again to make the 10th DICD the biggest, grandest, and most impactful edition yet.

Let us celebrate 10 years of academic excellence.
Let us shape the future of dermatology together.

We look forward to welcoming you to Udaipur.

With warm regards,
Team DICD
